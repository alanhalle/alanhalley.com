When I ran product acquisitions, vendors got two to three weeks to answer an RFP. Writing a proposal was real work, and the work told you who was serious.

This morning an Upwork job had 50+ proposals eleven minutes after it was posted. AI wrote them. They all sound qualified.

Writing became free, so it stopped being evidence. What I do now when hiring: wait three days for the bots to pass, ask one screening question, believe only what I can click on, and start with a small paid milestone.
