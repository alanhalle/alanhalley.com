France, 1982: Going Home

Part 5 of 5.

France · 1982 · October 2026
Back to writing

I asked if I could continue working at Les Renardières and was told that it was impossible to get another visa extension. Frenchmen were out of work.

In November I tried to sell the Nissan that I bought on the street in Paris. None of the French guys wanted it because it was orange, and they had no place to park it, and it had Luxembourg plates. And then I caught a cold after work at Les Renardières ended.

I was planning to go to Germany for Christmas and then decide where to go next. I had a 30-day Eurail pass. After a few days in bed under a pile of blankets, I went to TWA on the Champs-Élysées and told a clerk, "I want to go home."

"And where is home, sir?"

"St. Louis."

"When would you like to depart?"

"Tomorrow."
