I usually write a status report on Fridays. I did that for years as a project manager. I still do it. For myself now.

[This year I decided I didn't need a project management system. I'd built a simple issue tracker and stopped using it. Then, this week, I ended up with one anyway.]

## Basecamp

I've followed Jason Fried and DHH for years. [In March, 37signals opened Basecamp to AI agents, and now both Claude and ChatGPT connect to it directly.] I could envision one monitor with Claude running and another with Basecamp. I thought Basecamp might make a good dashboard.

Then I looked at the prices. [Basecamp charges by the number of projects: one free, three for freelancers, eight, twelve, unlimited.] I have a lot of small projects. Basecamp is not solopreneur friendly.

Basecamp provides a pretty UI for humans, but a Nimbalyst canvas could provide the same dashboard. Or build your own project management system.

## The board

[So I asked Claude for a canvas. It came back with seven cards: the year's timeline, my outreach, my posting schedule, who I'm waiting on, essay ideas, money, and an allergy log. Each card was a file Claude was already keeping for me.]

[The live canvas showed each file at full length, in small type. The picture Claude made of it was easier to read.] The .png is much easier for me to read. [So the board I use is a single page Claude refreshes whenever one of those files changes.]

I got my custom project management system, after all!

## No blank page

I didn't have to decide what to put on the canvas. It reminds me of the notes apps I tried, where I was confronted with a blank structure. As Zoltai said: it's hard to automate a process you don't have.

[The board has no tasks, no assignments, no due-date reminders, no meetings. It's my Friday status report, kept current by someone else.]
