Excremedia

My most productive year, and the sewage I had to wade through to show anyone.

Software · AI · Marketing
Back to writing
excremedian.

Social media, from the point of view of someone trying to tell people what he built.

Coined by Alan Halley, November 3, 2026.

If I had gone to sleep in 1986 and awakened in 2026, this would seem like software hell. Unfortunately, it’s not a bad dream.

In December 1984 I finished my master’s in nuclear engineering. I mailed 200 letters to companies that might hire a nuclear engineer. I expected nothing. I got two interviews and two offers.

I used dice.com from 1996 to 2006 to find IT PM contracts. It was simple: Install new servers and content management system in San Jose, California, $85/hour. Apply button with my stored resume.

This year, I built 11 software projects (8 still live), published three physics papers on Zenodo, and wrote 26 essays. This has been my most productive year ever.

And this year I have tried all sorts of dysfunctional software to tell people about what I have done: excremedia.

My AI writes the code, runs the physics and keeps my records. It isn’t allowed to post for me: LinkedIn’s API terms forbid using the API “to automate posting.” I have to be confused by all the flashing lights and noise. I quit playing pinball machines years ago. They were more fun.

Hacker News killed my last three submissions on arrival. No reason given; I found out by logging out and looking. HN has a DOS-like interface. On a dead post there’s no comment box at all.

Mastodon disabled my account. No email, no explanation.

Fiverr and Upwork don’t work now. A new listing can’t be seen until it has the scores that only come from being seen. Toptal told me I was unqualified despite a PMP and 20 years of experience with nationally known organizations: Cisco, Hitachi, Los Alamos National Laboratory, the U.S. Army, General Motors.

YC Confounders doesn’t have a search. All the Confounders want to marry an engineer, but can’t even build an MVP, an afternoon’s work in 2026.

Now I’m AI-pilled, trudging through social media sewage. Wading through the sewage has been quite unpleasant.

Excremedia.
