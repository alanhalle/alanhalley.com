Just a year ago, I was not building software. I wasn't doing neutron transport calculations. I hadn't published any essays.

October 2025 to October 2026. Hover for details; click to open what came out of it. Best on a wide screen.

building in service dates from memory

October 2025: nothing.

November–December 2025: getting started. Second-brain research (Notion, Obsidian, YouTube) and a first test, Claude reorganizing a folder, both dated from memory. A first app, a tourist app built on Emergent, then prototypes there: an outliner, a mind map, a to-do list, the first Decisions.

February–March 2026: my own setup. A news bot and OpenClaw, then the second-brain repo, still in use. ilhe.us in February; in March the restaurant menus, Decisions, Open Brain and an issue tracker.

April–May: the first launches. briefwork.io and a Lean Canvas extension. The Chart of the Nuclides, built in a day. Memory, the wiki and Nimbalyst became how I work. The STARFIRE rerun started the physics. Essays started in April.

June–August: the busiest stretch. beachburbs, darthvader.mba, TrikeScore (another one-day build), the family tree research, the AI benchmark paper, the Sapetinga lot study, and the first LinkedIn posts.

September–October: publishing. All three physics papers on Zenodo (STARFIRE, the AI benchmark, slit-gap). Inbox triage, The High Road map, and the year's fastest pace of essays and posts.

In all: 11 software projects, 8 still live; 3 studies and maps; 3 physics papers; 26 essays; 20 LinkedIn posts.
