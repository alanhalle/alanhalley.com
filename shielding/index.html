Shielding calculations

OpenMC neutron and gamma shielding calculations, on contract. I do the calculation. The engineer or physicist who signs the report checks it.

OpenMC · Monte Carlo · $100 an hour

In 1984 I wrote a master's thesis at the University of Missouri on neutrons leaking through gaps in a fusion reactor shield, using Monte Carlo. Then I spent 40 years in software.

In 2026 I rebuilt that study in OpenMC and published it, then two more papers on the same problem. All three are open, with the code and data, so you can check my work before you hire me:

  • Gap streaming in the STARFIRE shield, with weight windows (plain-English version)
  • An empirical rule for streaming through bent slit gaps, from 32 geometries (plain-English version)
  • A benchmark of AI-written OpenMC inputs, nine models from three vendors (plain-English version)

What I do

  • Build an OpenMC model from your drawing or spec, in Python, that you own and can rerun.
  • Dose rate, flux or heating where you need it, with statistical uncertainties.
  • Weight windows when the shield is thick enough that plain Monte Carlo won't converge.
  • Parameter sweeps: thickness, material, gap width and offset.
  • Translate MCNP inputs you already have into OpenMC.
  • Review OpenMC models other people built.

What I don't do

I don't sign shielding reports. I'm not a licensed engineer or a qualified medical physicist. My calculations go to the person responsible for the design. No export-controlled or classified work. My geometry work so far is OpenMC's built-in geometry, not CAD; ask first about DAGMC.

Price

$100 an hour, billed for the hours actually worked. Fixed packages: a review of your OpenMC model, $200; a shielding calculation from your drawing, $750; a deep-penetration study with weight windows and a parameter sweep, $2,000.
